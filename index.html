Assessing Manufacturing Green Logistics Performance:
A Strategic Roadmap for the East Midlands
2026

This report presents the first regional assessment of green logistics (GL) performance across East Midlands manufacturing. Seven practice areas were measured — purchasing, end-of-life, transport, packaging, inventory, environmental management and reverse logistics — each scored 1 (not in place) to 5 (fully embedded). The report details every area and delivers a clear, evidence-based strategic roadmap.

Key Messages

Five Findings Frame the Report

Finding 1

Regional manufacturers sit mid-way on green logistics maturity — strong on packaging and environmental management, weakest on reverse logistics.

Finding 2

Size is the sharpest divide. Mid-sized firms outperform small firms by a full point on every practice area.

Finding 3

Firms with an environmental policy report substantially higher green-logistics maturity than firms without one (3.95 vs 2.88).

Finding 4

Training and practice adoption rise together, moving in lockstep across every practice area (r = 0.90).

Finding 5

Reverse logistics is the region's shared gap — the weakest area in three of the five priority sectors.
